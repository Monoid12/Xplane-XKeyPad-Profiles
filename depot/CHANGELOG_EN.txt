P_ Profiles for X-Plane 12 – changelog
(Deutsch: siehe CHANGELOG.txt)

Version 3.9 – 28 November 2026
  - FlightFactor 757 and 767 (757-200, 757-300, 757 C-32, 757-RF, 767-200ER, 767-300ER, 767-F, 767-SF and 767-400ER): the autopilot keys now show their lamp like on
    the MCP – LNAV, VNAV, FL CH, HDG HOLD, V/S, ALT HOLD, LOC, APP, THR, SPD and A/P L/C/R light up green when the
    mode is selected (XL and Stream Deck+).
  - FlightFactor 757 and 767: EICAS COMPUTER shows the positions correctly (L/AUTO/R, before AUTO/L/R).
  - FlightFactor 757 and 767: PACK L/R shows the standby positions in the order of the selector (N, C, W; before, C and
    N were swapped).
  - FlightFactor 757 and 767: the WINDOW HEAT keys carry the panel labels (L SIDE, L FWD, R FWD, R SIDE instead of
    L1, L2, R1, R2).
  - FlightFactor 757 and 767 – Stream Deck+: the ALT dial has two step sizes. Tapping the display switches between
    100 ft and 1000 ft (title ALT 1000); in 1000 mode each detent jumps to the next full thousand. P_B777_XKeyPad.lua
    has been extended for this.

Version 3.8 – 23 November 2026
  - Zibo 737-800 and LevelUp 737, Stream Deck+: the IAS dial shows MACH with three digits (before: as a whole number),
    the display follows C/O.
  - Zibo 737-800 and LevelUp 737, Stream Deck+: tapping BARO switches inHg/hPa, the display follows the EFIS switch.
  - Zibo 737-800 and LevelUp 737, Stream Deck+: MINS – turning changes the minimums, tapping switches RADIO/BARO, the
    display shows MINS RADIO or MINS BARO.
  - Zibo 737-800 and LevelUp 737: new keys on DOORS – CARGO AFT, COCKPIT DOOR, AIRSTAIRS and the overwing exits
    OVWG L/R FWD and AFT (737-600/-700: OVWG L/R AFT only).
  - Zibo 737-800 and LevelUp 737: EMER EXIT LT shows OFF/ARM/ON; ARM closes the guard, a long press selects ON.

Version 3.7.2 – 22 November 2026
  - Diamond DA40 NG: the XL pages PFD and MFD are laid out like the G1000 bezel – NAV FF, NAV 1/2, HDG SYNC on the
    left, COM FF, COM 1/2, CRS SYNC, PAN PUSH, FMS PUSH next to D→/MENU, FPL/PROC, CLR/ENT on the right, map arrows
    and softkeys 1–12 in the middle. The knobs are on the Stream Deck+.
  - Diamond DA40 NG: GFC 700 page like the MFD bezel (AP, FD, NAV with HDG, APR; ALT, VS, FLC with VNV, NOSE UP,
    NOSE DN), plus TOGA and AP DISC. AUDIO like the audio panel (MIC left, MON right) with a new MON DME key.
  - Diamond DA40 NG: new keys PFD WINDOW, MFD WINDOW, REVERSION and STBY BARO; Stream Deck+: tapping BARO switches
    inHg/hPa.

Version 3.7.1 – 22 November 2026
  - Stream Deck+, aircraft with GNS or G1000 pages (A36, C172, C208, CJ525, DA40NG, G36, King Air 350, PA28, Phenom 300, Seneca, V35B): the GNS, PFD and MFD selection
    keys on the XL open the matching page on the Stream Deck+ (before, e.g. the radio page).
  - COM 2 on the Stream Deck+ now also for C152, C172, C208, CJ525 and V35B (new page COM 2) and for DA40NG, G36
    and Phenom 300 (new page COM 1/2).
  - Bonanza A36 (PAE): the Glass version loads its configuration again (STBY VAC only in the Analog version).
    Stream Deck+: radio page with COM 1 and COM 2, BARO and OBS 2 on their own page, tapping BARO switches
    inHg/hPa (X-Plane pressure unit), PROP shows the lever position.

Version 3.7 – 22 November 2026
  - All profiles, Stream Deck+: the COM dials show the active frequency on the left and the standby frequency
    on the right, labelled ACTIVE and STANDBY; pressing swaps them (COM 1 and COM 2).
  - Falcon 8X: annunciator pushbuttons show both legends as in the cockpit, with original text and colour
    (e.g. NAV top LOGO, bottom OFF; BOOST top ST-BY, bottom OFF; autopilot active green, armed blue).
  - Falcon 8X: Stream Deck+ – tapping BARO 1, BARO 2 or SFD SET switches all altimeters together between hPa
    and inHg; the display follows the unit. New pages brightness displays, panel, lights and HUD.
  - Falcon 8X: DISPLAYS page ordered (displays left to right on top, tablets below).
  - Falcon 8X: new keys FIRE REAR/BACK (baggage fire), RAT RESET, PAX OXYGEN; reworked X-Camera views EFB,
    FMC, Pedestal 2 and PFD ND.
  - Falcon 8X: COM dials on the Stream Deck+ use the Falcon's own radio knobs.

Version 3.6 – 21 November 2026
  - Backup and restore: your own X-KeyPad configurations and X-Camera files that the installer replaces are
    kept permanently as originals. New: Wiederherstellen_macOS.command or Wiederherstellen_Windows.bat restores
    the original state before the P_ Profiles or a backup.

Version 3.5 – 21 November 2026
  - Keys as in the cockpit: on every page the keys are arranged the way the switches sit in the cockpit of the
    respective aircraft – including autopilot and EFIS (taken from the 3D cockpits). Unchanged: FlightFactor
    (already arranged this way), ToLiss A319–A321 (by hand), DOORS (fuselage from above), home page.
  - Language selectable: Deutsch or English before installing (English: PREV, NEXT, MUTE, DEAF, BACK).
  - Discord keys MUTE/DEAF can be hidden in the installer.
  - Folder P_Profile now lives in X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins; the installer moves it there.
  - ToLiss: MFD, Fire and XPDR folders in the common design; Engines with stacked thrust detents,
    TAXI/REV next to them (A320 family with a new REV key); Air/Bleed like the AIR COND panel.
  - Plugins selectable: X-Camera, Better Pushback, SimLoad, AviTab and xPilot are no longer required. The
    installer detects them in the Resources/plugins folder and ticks them in the window. An unticked plugin
    is left out: its Stream Deck keys and folders that became empty are removed (the space stays empty), its
    commands no longer do anything in the X-KeyPad configuration – so the configuration loads without the
    plugin too. The choice also applies to later updates.

Version 3.4 – 21 November 2026
  - New: Embraer E-Jets by X-Crafts – E170, E175, E190, E195 and Lineage 1000, each with its own profile
    (P_E170X, P_E175X, P_E190X, P_E195X, P_LINX) and X-Camera views 1–15; Lineage with a cabin page.
  - VOR/ZURÜCK circle sorted by type (E175 FPS → E175 X etc.); unique type key "E190 FPS" /
    "E190 X-CRAFTS" on the XL start page.

Version 3.3 – 21 November 2026
  - Airbus A330-300 (Laminar): doors page with all 12 doors; NOSE shows OFF/TAXI/T.O.; BARO on the Plus
    sets captain and first officer together (press STD/QNH, tap hPa/inHg), BARO 2 removed; FCU dials as
    on ToLiss: short press = push, long press = pull.
  - New FlyWithLua script P_A333L_Baro.lua (required for the Laminar A330, preselected in the installer).

Version 3.2 – 17 November 2026
  - New installer window: profiles (with status installed / aircraft found / not found), Lua scripts,
    AeroACARS and automatic updates in ONE window instead of several dialogs – for installation and for
    every automatic update. Quick selection "All found" / "Installed only"; newly ticked aircraft tick
    their required Lua scripts too. AeroACARS can now also be changed during an update.

Version 3.1 – 17 November 2026
  - Automatic updates: when the SkunkCrafts Updater brings the folder "P_Profile" to a new version, a
    window with your installed profiles opens by itself afterwards; you can add more, and "Install"
    installs everything (profiles, configurations, camera views, Lua scripts). While X-Plane is running
    it waits until X-Plane has been quit; "Later" asks again after 12 hours.
    For this the installer sets up an update helper on request (run the installer once by hand).
  - Installer: finds the X-Plane folder when started from "X-Plane 12/P_Profile"; detects X-Plane by its
    exact process name; waits more reliably for the Stream Deck software to quit.

Version 3.0.1 – 17 November 2026
  - Simple Ground Equipment & Services (SGES) removed: the GROUND folder and its 17 keys are gone from
    all profiles and X-KeyPad configurations; SGES is no longer a prerequisite.
  - Start page: SIMLOAD and PUSHBACK each move one place to the left.
  - FPS E175/E190/E195: the door keys now actually move the doors (before, they only switched a
    display value of the EFB). DOORS page with all six doors: DOOR L1, DOOR R1, DOOR L2,
    DOOR R2 (service door), CARGO FWD and CARGO AFT.
